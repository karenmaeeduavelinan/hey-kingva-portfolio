THE SERVICES BEHIND THE STRATEGY

From the first idea to the final post.

01

Discovery & Brand Audit

  • ✓Understand your business, products/services, and goals
  • ✓Review existing social media presence and performance
  • ✓Assess brand identity (voice, style, visuals) and key differentiators
  • ✓Align on scope, deliverables, and KPIs
02

Market & Industry Research

  • ✓Analyze industry trends and emerging topics
  • ✓Identify market demand and customer pain points
  • ✓Research relevant keywords, hashtags, and content opportunities
  • ✓Spot seasonal events and industry timing for relevant campaigns
03

Target Audience Research

  • ✓Define ideal customer profiles (e.g., demographics, location, interests, behavior)
  • ✓Understand their needs, challenges, and decision triggers
  • ✓Identify the best platforms and content formats they engage with
  • ✓Map the customer journey (awareness → consideration → action)
04

Competitor Analysis

  • ✓Identify key competitors (direct and indirect)
  • ✓Analyze their content (topics, format, frequency, and style)
  • ✓Review engagement, audience growth, and top-performing posts
  • ✓Find content gaps, opportunities, and ways to differentiate your brand
05

Strategy Development

  • ✓Set clear and measurable objectives (e.g., awareness, engagement, leads)
  • ✓Define content pillars and key themes
  • ✓Create a content and campaign strategy (with aligned messaging and CTAs)
  • ✓Establish platform-specific approaches (e.g., FB, IG, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube)
  • ✓Set success metrics (KPIs) for tracking
06

Content Planning & Calendar

  • ✓Build a monthly content calendar (weekly/daily posting plan)
  • ✓Map content by pillar, platform, and campaign
  • ✓Plan post types (graphics, videos, reels, carousels, blogs, etc.)
  • ✓Include key dates (holidays, events, product launches) and promotions
07

Content Creation & Asset Production

  • ✓Create on-brand graphics and marketing assets (Canva Pro)
  • ✓Edit short-form and long-form videos (CapCut Pro)
  • ✓Write compelling, platform-optimized captions and CTAs
  • ✓Prepare supporting assets (thumbnails, subtitles, hashtags, and links)
08

Review, Approval & Final Preparation

  • ✓Quality check for accuracy, branding, and platform requirements
  • ✓Confirm captions, hashtags, and links
  • ✓Ensure videos/graphics meet specs and best practices
  • ✓Send for client review and approval
  • ✓Prepare final files and schedule in the content tool
09

Scheduling & Publishing

  • ✓Schedule content using Meta Business Suite or your preferred tool
  • ✓Publish across relevant platforms (FB, IG, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, etc.)
  • ✓Ensure proper formatting, tagging, and platform optimization
  • ✓Monitor for successful posting and initial engagement
10

Performance Analysis & Optimization

  • ✓Track key metrics (reach, impressions, engagement, clicks, leads, conversions)
  • ✓Analyze what's working and what's not (content, timing, format, messaging)
  • ✓Provide a performance report (weekly or monthly)
  • ✓Refine and optimize the content strategy for continuous growth and better results
